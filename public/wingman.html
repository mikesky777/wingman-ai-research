Thesis vs Evidence vs Entry

Three independent judgements. A strong thesis is not a signal to buy now.

Thesis Score — 0–100

How attractive the token itself is: meme quality, catalyst, distribution, liquidity, dev integrity, chart, mindshare and valuation.

Evidence Confidence — 0–100

How complete and trustworthy the underlying research is. A token can score Thesis 82 / Evidence 46 — interesting, but unverified.

Entry Score — 0–10

Whether the current chart offers a good place to take risk. Thesis 88 with Entry 3 and state EXTENDED means wait, not buy.
